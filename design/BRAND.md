# Infinium — brand brief for document design

Purpose: give this file to Claude (or any designer) to produce on-brand
collateral — whitepapers, reports, one-pagers, decks. It is self-contained:
everything needed is in this document. Derived from the production website
(infinium-technology.com) and its design system, 2026-07.

---

## 1. Who this is

**Infinium Technology** — financial-services-only technology and consulting
firm. Head office Amsterdam (**Infinium Consulting B.V.**, Fred Roeskestraat
115); Middle East office in the DIFC, Dubai (**Infinium Technology Ltd**,
Innovation One). Serves banks, insurers, asset managers and fintechs across
the EU, Nordics and GCC.

- Positioning line: **"Engineering with context."**
- Homepage promise: *From complex systems to confident decisions.*
- Stance: a specialist alternative to the big-brand consultancies —
  practitioners, not career consultants; measurable outcomes, not slideware.
- Products: **QBricks** (governed, AI-ready data products) and **VBricks**
  (engineered model testing; pre-launch). Partners: Databricks, Microsoft
  Fabric, Alteryx, Appian, Quantexa (technology); NextWave Consulting (UK),
  Aspiro Management Consultants (GCC) (consulting).

**Visual thesis: "Refraction under pressure — a data estate rendered as
optical glass."** Every illustration is an optical event: light entering
disorder and leaving as structure.

## 2. Colour

Light-first. White and cool mineral neutrals are the canvas; cobalt is
earned, appearing for emphasis, actions and data — never as wallpaper.

| Role | Name | Hex | Document use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page canvas | Void | `#F7F9FC` | page background (or pure white for print) |
| Raised surface tint | Abyss | `#DFE8F4` | panel fills at 20–45% over white |
| Headings / wordmark | Paper | `#102B50` | H1–H3, cover titles |
| Body copy | Glass | `#2A415D` | running text |
| Supporting copy | Ice | `#3E526B` | intros, captions; hairline source |
| Muted labels | Steel | `#5B6B7F` | eyebrows, footers, page numbers |
| Deep brand | Navy | `#173866` | charts, dark fills, quotes |
| Primary accent | Cobalt | `#234FBD` | key stats, links, chart emphasis |
| Interactive / focus | Signal | `#1B57C8` | links in digital PDFs |
| Image scrim | Overlay | `#07152F` | gradients over photography only |
| On-dark text | — | `#FFFFFF` / `#D8E5F4` / accent `#9BC4FF` | text on navy/overlay |

Rules:
- One accent: cobalt. No greens, oranges, purples, gradients-as-decoration.
- **QBricks red (`#D6111F` / `#FF4A50`) is the single exception** — used only
  in QBricks product contexts, never alongside general Infinium content.
- Hairlines: 1px, Ice at ~16% opacity. Use them generously as structure —
  between sections, around cells, under captions.
- Dark moments (navy/overlay panels) are allowed and welcome in documents —
  use for pull-quotes, section dividers, the cover.

## 3. Typography

One engineered family carries everything (client-directed: **no monospace**).

- **Geist** (variable, 100–900) — all text: headings, body, labels, tables.
  Open licence (SIL OFL), freely embeddable.
- **Outfit** (variable) — display moments only: the cover title, section
  numerals. It echoes the logo's geometric, monoline letterforms. Use
  sparingly — one display setting per spread at most.
- **Quicksand** — ONLY for the QBricks wordmark (bold "Q" in `#FF3A26`,
  regular "Bricks" in Paper). Never uppercase QBricks. Never set body copy
  in Quicksand.

Document scale (print, A4):
- Cover title: Outfit or Geist 600, 34–44pt, Paper, tracking −0.02em
- H1 section: Geist 600, 22–26pt, Paper
- H2: Geist 600, 15–17pt, Paper
- Eyebrow/kicker: Geist 500, 8–9pt, ALL CAPS, tracking +0.08–0.14em, Steel
  or Signal — this small tracked-caps label over a heading is the single
  most recognisable Infinium typographic gesture; use it on every section.
- Body: Geist 400, 9.5–11pt, Glass, line-height ~1.6, ragged right
- Captions/footnotes: Geist 400, 7.5–8pt, Steel
- Big statistics: Geist 300–400, 28–40pt, Paper, with the label in
  eyebrow style beneath (see §5)

## 4. Language

- **British English** (organised, authorised, centre).
- Sentence case for headings; never title case, never all-caps except
  eyebrows.
- Brand casing exactly: Infinium, QBricks, VBricks, Databricks, Microsoft
  Fabric, Alteryx, Appian, Quantexa, NextWave, Aspiro, EcoVadis.
- Canonical service names: Strategy and Change · Transformation · Digital &
  automation · Data & AI · Regulation & compliance · Sustainable finance.
- Voice: confident, concrete, engineering-led. Short declaratives. Outcomes
  and numbers over adjectives. No "leverage/synergy/best-in-class" filler.
  House flavour: "measurable outcomes, not slideware", "engineering with
  context", "hours, not months or years".
- Legal footer on documents: © Infinium Consulting B.V. Name the correct
  entity per region (B.V. for EU material, Infinium Technology Ltd for GCC).

## 5. Layout idioms (what makes a page look like Infinium)

- Generous white space; a 12-column mindset; content column ≤ ~66ch.
- **Ruled cells**: stats and credentials sit in bordered grids with 1px
  hairlines and near-white fills — a strip of 3–4 cells, each with a big
  numeral and a tracked-caps label (e.g. "22 / LEADING GLOBAL FS CLIENTS").
- **Eyebrow → heading → body** is the universal section pattern.
- Figures and photos sit in hairline-bordered frames with an eyebrow-style
  caption row beneath, separated by a hairline.
- Pull-quotes or key findings may run white-on-navy as full-width bands —
  the one deliberate dark moment per few pages.
- Isometric cube motifs (the voxel mark, 2:1 isometric cubes in cobalt/
  navy/clear glass) may punctuate section openers as small marginalia.

## 6. Imagery

Two families, never mixed in one frame:

**A. Generated glass renders** (illustrative). Light-studio recipe —
append to every generation prompt:

> Editorial product photography on a pure white seamless studio background.
> Soft large-source key light from the upper left with even fill; one soft
> contact shadow grounds the subject. Every element is cobalt blue, deep
> navy or clear optical glass. Cool colour temperature throughout. Crisp
> focus with shallow depth-of-field falloff. Shot on a Phase One with a
> 120mm macro. Cinematic and restrained. No amber, no gold, no orange, no
> warm tones, no purple, no green, no marble, no granite, no speckled
> stone, no ceramic, no tableware, no flat illustration, no bar charts, no
> text, no logos, no watermarks, no people, no user interfaces, no charts,
> no diagrams, no neon, no lens flare.

Subjects are sculptural metaphors, one per image: plates snapping into
registration, scattered cubes resolving into a block, one cobalt sphere
singled out among clear ones. (Model used in production:
`bfl/flux-1.1-pro-ultra`, raw mode. The negatives above are earned — the
model otherwise drifts to marble, warm tones and literal charts.)

**Never in generated imagery:** people, faces, text, UIs, circuit boards,
glowing brains, neon, skylines, hooded hackers, stock composition.

**B. Real photography** (people, offices, events). Honest and warm; used
for culture, teams, offices. Hard rules: never AI-upscale a photograph
whose faces are smaller than ~80px in the source — upscalers redraw small
faces. Face-enhancement passes are forbidden on real people. If a photo is
too small, show it smaller; don't invent pixels.

## 7. Logo and marks

- Wordmark "Infinium" with the faceted glass-cube mark (ice-glass top,
  deep-navy left face, cobalt right face). On light backgrounds use the
  full-colour mark; on navy use white text with the colour mark.
- Clearspace ≥ the cube's own width on all sides; don't recolour, outline,
  rotate or add effects.
- Partner/credential logos sit in ruled cells (see §5), sized by **optical
  area**, not equal height — a wide wordmark at the same height as a square
  seal reads three times heavier.

## 9. Whitepaper skeleton

1. **Cover** — Void or white canvas; eyebrow (series name / date), Outfit
   display title in Paper, one glass render or the voxel mark, logo bottom-
   left, entity line bottom-right.
2. **Inside** — executive summary with 2–3 ruled stat cells; eyebrow-led
   sections; one navy pull-quote band; figures in hairline frames with
   caption rows; charts in cobalt/navy/ice on white with hairline gridlines.
3. **Back** — offices (both entities + addresses), contact
   (sales@infinium.technology), partner logo strip in ruled cells, legal
   line.

**Don'ts:** no gradients-as-decoration, no drop shadows heavier than a
whisper, no rounded-corner card soup, no stock photography, no clip-art
icons, no second accent colour, no title case, no QBricks red outside
QBricks material.
